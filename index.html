01

Profile

A mathematician working on convergence and mathematical structures under uncertainty.

Dr. Birojit Das is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at NIT Agartala, where he joined on 15 December 2025. His research interests include uncertainty theory, credibility theory, sequence spaces, and fuzzy topological structures.

He previously served as Assistant Professor at Amity University Kolkata, and as ad-hoc faculty at NIT Warangal, NIT Jalandhar, and Tripura University.
